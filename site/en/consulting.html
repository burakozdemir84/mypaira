Our Approach

We assess every organization in three dimensions

PAIRA's signature assessment framework reads AI readiness in three dimensions. The same framework is the skeleton of the Readiness Score.

01

Efficiency and Capacity

Where does your team's time go? Which tasks speed up with AI, and which should stay human? This is where the snapshot of capacity is taken.

02

Risk and Compliance

Data rules, confidentiality obligations, regulation and safe-use reflexes. This is where we draw the line of courage.

03

Competition and Positioning

Where do you stand before clients and the market? This is where the transformation's distinct value and narrative become clear.

If you wonder where you stand in these three dimensions, the Readiness Score gives a first answer in three minutes.
