Legal in the Loop Build your team's capability with our training

A four-module AI training programme for law firms. From understanding the models to using them safely, from everyday productivity to agent-run workflows, step by step. As one Master Class or module by module, according to your needs.

Plan a training for your firm Explore the modules
Programme

Four modules, two formats

01

AI Literacy

Understanding how the models work, where they are strong and where their limits lie, through a lawyer's eyes.

02

AI Governance

Usage policy, risk management and safe use within the framework of KVKK, GDPR and the EU AI Act.

03

AI Productivity

Gaining time in daily work by using the tools well for research, drafting and review.

04

AI Agents & Automation

Designing how repetitive workflows are handed over to agents and automations.
